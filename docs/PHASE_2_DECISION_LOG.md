# Phase 2 Activity & Event Tracking Decision Log

| decision_area | question | proposed_default | final_decision | owner | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mentor meeting definition | What counts as an official mentor meeting? | A recorded meeting between matched mentor and mentee, confirmed by date and recap link. | One recap represents exactly one mentor-mentee meeting. | VAM core team | One recap row should not combine multiple meetings. |
| Recap requirement | How often should mentees submit recap evidence? | At least one recap per mentee per month. | Each mentee is expected to submit at least one recap per month. | VAM core team | Monthly tracking is the operating baseline. |
| Multiple meetings | Can one recap represent multiple meetings? | No. | No. One recap cannot represent multiple meetings in Phase 2 MVP. | VAM core team | Create separate rows for separate meeting dates. |
| Recap link owner | Who is responsible for copying recap links? | Admin/core team records links from the Facebook group during operations review. | Admin/core team records date and Facebook recap link. | VAM core team | No mentee portal yet. |
| Missing recap follow-up | When should a missing recap trigger follow-up? | After two consecutive months without recap. | Create follow-up flag only after two consecutive months without recap. | VAM core team | Do not flag after only one missing month. |
| Meeting frequency | What is the expected meeting frequency by month? | At least one recap per mentee per month. | At least one recap per mentee per month. | VAM core team | This defines monthly recap coverage. |
| Event scope | Which events/trainings are tracked in Phase 2? | Mentee Orientation, Kickoff, Tổng kết. | Mentee Orientation, Kickoff, Tổng kết. | VAM core team | Other events are out of scope for Phase 2 MVP. |
| Attendance population | Should event attendance include mentors, mentees, speakers, and organizers? | Support role via `role_at_event`. | Support role via `role_at_event`, with reporting focused on required event participation. | VAM core team | Keeps template flexible without expanding MVP scope. |
| Registration status | What registration statuses are allowed? | `registered`, `unknown`. | `registered`, `unknown`. | VAM core team | Simplified for MVP. |
| Attendance status | What attendance statuses are allowed? | `attended`, `registered_absent`. | `attended` = Tham dự; `registered_absent` = Đăng ký nhưng không tham dự. | VAM core team | No separate absent-with-notice status in MVP. |
| Event recap requirement | Should event recap links be required? | Optional. | Optional. | VAM core team | Event attendance can exist without recap URL. |
| Monthly reports | What monthly KPIs does founder/core team need? | Recaps, active mentees, active mentors, events, attendance, feedback. | Recap count; active mentee count; active mentor count; number of trainings/events; attendance count; feedback count. | VAM core team | Feedback count should be included if feedback data exists. |
| Active mentee metric | What counts as active mentee for monthly report? | Mentee with at least one recap in the reporting month. | Mentee with recorded activity/recap in the reporting month for MVP reporting. | VAM core team | Can be refined later with admin override. |
| Active mentor metric | What counts as active mentor for monthly report? | Mentor with at least one mentee recap in the reporting month. | Mentor with at least one mentee recap/activity in the reporting month for MVP reporting. | VAM core team | Can be refined later with admin override. |
| Required recap fields | Which mentoring recap fields are required? | season_code, meeting_date, meeting_month, recap_url, mentee identifier, and mentor/match identifier. | Use the finalized recap template; require enough identifiers to resolve mentee and mentor/match. | VAM core team | Identifier can be email, code, or match_id. |
| Required event fields | Which event participation fields are required? | season_code, event_name or event_code, event_date, person identifier, attendance_status. | Use the finalized event participation template with simplified statuses. | VAM core team | Registration status can be unknown. |
| Privacy | Should full Facebook recap content be copied into VAM OS? | No; store URL only for MVP. | No. Store recap URL only. | VAM core team | Reduces privacy and moderation risk. |
| Implementation timing | Should schema be implemented now? | Wait until technical design review. | Do not create migrations until schema is reviewed against SOP. | VAM technical team | Current task is documentation/template finalization only. |
